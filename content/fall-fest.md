# Franklin Fall Fest — Saturday, October 24, 2026

![Franklin Fall Fest flyer: October 24, 2026, 1:00 to 3:00 PM, Franklin Commons](assets/images/fall-fest-flyer.jpg)

**A new tradition: welcome to Franklin Fall Fest!**

## Event Details

- **When:** Saturday, October 24, 2026, 1:00–3:00 p.m.
- **Where:** Franklin Commons

## A New Tradition

This year we're transitioning from our annual Trunk or Treat event to the all-new Franklin Fall Fest! While Trunk or Treat has been a wonderful tradition, we wanted to broaden the celebration to offer even more fun for the whole family.

## What to Expect

Franklin Fall Fest will feature a wider variety of activities, creating a festive autumn experience that everyone can enjoy:

- 🎨 Crafts
- 🎵 Music
- 🍬 Treats
- 🎮 Games

More details are coming soon. Watch for them in the next PTA newsletter!
