# Franklin Math Program

![Franklin Math Program 2026-2027 flyer](assets/images/math_program.png)

The Franklin Math program is run by our Franklin PTA and consists of two parts:

## Math Challenge Bi-Weekly Program

15 sets of challenging problems throughout the year, free and open to all students.

Chairpersons: Subarna Kar & Gia Shen — [mathchallenge@mybenfranklinpta.org](mailto:mathchallenge@mybenfranklinpta.org)

See the [Math Challenge](math-challenge.html) page for the full schedule and submission links.

## Math Contests

Opportunities for students to showcase their math skills: Noetic Learning Math Contest, Math Kangaroo Team, Math Kangaroo (Individual), and Math Is Cool.

Chairpersons: Sabrina Chen & Moli Zhang — [mathcontest@mybenfranklinpta.org](mailto:mathcontest@mybenfranklinpta.org)

See the [Math Contests](math-contest.html) page for dates, eligibility, and registration details.
