# Become a PTA Member Today!

We offer two levels of PTA general membership, renewed annually. Join now to become a member for the 2026–2027 school year.

## Individual Membership — $18

One member login, access to the member directory, one vote at general membership meetings, and exclusive discounts at Washington State Fair, Great Wolf Lodge, Wild Waves, and more.

## Family Membership — $30

Two member logins, access to the member directory, two votes at general membership meetings, and the same exclusive discounts.

Membership is low commitment. Members aren't required to attend meetings, serve on a committee, or volunteer at events. But if you'd like to meet other parents and get more involved, we'd love to have you — there are plenty of ways to participate.

**How does the PTA raise funds?** Through membership dues and school fundraising events. This funding lets the PTA provide events, programs, and grants that local and state funding doesn't cover.

Membership scholarships are available — contact the school counselor or reach out to [president@mybenfranklinpta.org](mailto:president@mybenfranklinpta.org) for more information.
