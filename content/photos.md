# Photos

A look back at recent PTA events.

## Campus Clean-Up Party

Franklin families turned out before the first day of school to spruce up our campus — weeding, pruning, planting, sweeping, and spreading wood chips. Thanks to everyone who brought gloves, rakes, and leaf blowers and pitched in — the school looked great for the first day!

## Ice Cream Social

Our back-to-school Ice Cream Social was a great way to kick off the year — free ice cream for kids, PTA membership sign-ups, spiritwear, and a community art activity. Thank you to everyone who came out and to the volunteers who ran the booths!
