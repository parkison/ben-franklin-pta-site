# Reflections Art Competition 2026-2027

**Theme: "What My Culture Means to Me"** — a National PTA arts competition. **Submissions are due Friday, November 6, 2026.**

![Reflections 2026-2027 flyer](assets/images/reflections-flyer.png)

## Important details

- You will need to turn in your artwork AND a [student entry form](https://benfranklinpta.sharepoint.com/:b:/s/pta/IQCWeTvH-rZ2TIIYA4FzGId8AQkri3tTd_RpfWgzo0Eveuk?e=k5IND6) to your teacher.
- Your artwork CANNOT have your name on the public facing side of your artwork. Please write it on the back.

## Categories

A student may submit an entry in any of six categories:

- [Literature](https://lwptsa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/25.26-literature-entry-guidelines.pdf) — Works of fiction, nonfiction, poetry, prose, drama, short stories and lyrics.
- [Music Composition](https://lwptsa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/25.26-music-entry-guidelines.pdf) — Original musical composition, with or without words. Notation/score is required for music composition. File must be in MP3 or WAV (NOT MP4).
- [Photography](https://lwptsa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/25.26-photo-entry-guidelines.pdf) — Color or black and white, included techniques: photogram, negative sandwich, and multiple exposure. Check quality and sizing minimums: 640x960 pixels and 300dpi resolution.
- [Visual Arts](https://lwptsa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/25.26-VA-entry-guidelines.pdf) — Drawing, painting in tempera, oil, acrylic, watercolor & computer generated art, two-dimensional collage & mixed media, printmaking. LWPTSA council is not accepting 3D art. Please no stretched canvas more than ¾ in. and please no framed art. Check quality and sizing minimums: 640x960 pixels and 300dpi resolution.
- [Film/Video](https://lwptsa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/25.26-film-entry-guidelines.pdf) — Original works, with or without sound, of animation, narrative, documentary, experimental or music video/film. Must not exceed 5min in length and 1,000 MB in file size in MP4, MOV or AVI format.
- [Choreography/Dance](https://lwptsa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/25.26-dance-entry-guidelines.pdf) — Originally choreographed dance composition. Must not exceed 5min in length and 1,000 MB in file size in MP4, MOV or AVI format.

## Accessible Arts

For artists needing accommodations to participate, the [Accessible Arts Division](https://lwptsa.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/accessible-arts-guidelines_eng-f-2-1.pdf) is an option for students with a disability as defined under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).
