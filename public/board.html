Interested in Joining the Board or Chairing a Committee?

We can always do more with more willing hands! Please consider donating your time by joining our board or chairing one of our many positions. Board positions span the school year, but many chairperson positions are tied to a specific activity or event and don't require a full-year commitment.

Not sure what would be a good fit? Reach out to president@mybenfranklinpta.org — we're happy to chat by phone, email, or in person and answer any questions.

See the PTA Mission page for more on what board and chairperson roles involve.
