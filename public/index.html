Welcome Back, Eagles!

We are excited to welcome all new and returning families to Ben Franklin for the 2026–2027 school year! The Ben Franklin PTA has been working hard behind-the-scenes to prepare for a great year for all students, staff, and the Franklin community. We're so glad you're here and look forward to meeting you!

The Ben Franklin PTA is looking forward to continuing to support the Franklin community this year. We have many ways that you can get involved – join the PTA, volunteer your time, donate funds, and sign up for our mailing list and Facebook page to stay informed about what the PTA is up to and opportunities for you to get involved.

Show your Franklin pride! The Back-to-School Spiritwear Sale is in its final weeks, with up to 35% off sitewide. Shop Spirit Wear.
