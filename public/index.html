Welcome Back, Eagles!

We are excited to welcome all new and returning families to Ben Franklin for the 2026–2027 school year! The Ben Franklin PTA has been working hard behind-the-scenes to prepare for a great year for all students, staff, and the Franklin community. We're so glad you're here and look forward to meeting you!

The Ben Franklin PTA is looking forward to continuing to support the Franklin community this year. We have many ways that you can get involved – join the PTA, volunteer your time, donate funds, and sign up for our mailing list and Facebook page to stay informed about what the PTA is up to and opportunities for you to get involved.

Show your Franklin pride! The Back-to-School Spiritwear Sale is in its final weeks, with up to 35% off sitewide. Shop Spirit Wear.

Announcements

Thank you for an amazing Ninja Warrior Run! Thanks to our incredible community, we exceeded our fundraising goal — and yes, that means Carnival is coming back in the spring! A huge thank-you to every donor and to the 58 volunteers who set up, ran, and cleaned up the obstacle course. Read more

Special General Membership Meeting — Tuesday, November 17, 6:00 p.m. (online). PTA members will vote on a $6,659 EZ Grant for the Drama Club. We need at least 10 members for a quorum, so please attend! The Zoom link is in the PTA newsletter.

International Night — Thursday, November 19, 6:00–8:00 p.m. About 12 countries are signed up, plus a Lion Dance performance! We still need help with setup and takedown. Event details

Pantry Pack Food Drive — November 16 to December 1. Donate small, individually packed foods (mac & cheese cups, Top Ramen, granola bars, fruit cups, crackers, instant oatmeal) to help feed students in our district. Questions? Email lapierce@lwsd.org or bbaker@lwsd.org.

Winter Toy Drive — through December 10. The Lake Washington Schools Foundation is collecting new, unwrapped toys for ages 5–18, especially teens. Donate or find drop-off locations

Hearing and Vision Screening — Thursday, December 3. The school nurse needs parent volunteers from 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. No medical background is needed, but you must be an LWSD-approved volunteer. Sign up to volunteer
