Math Challenge Bi-Weekly Program

Math Challenge Bi-Weekly program is a FREE bi-weekly math program that is open to ALL Ben Franklin Elementary students. No registration is necessary! Visit the Math Challenge website for more details.

Students will receive 15 problem sets throughout the school year — one every two weeks. Each problem set has a specific deadline, and students are encouraged to complete as many as they can. Participants may choose to submit their work online or turn it in at the library.

Important: Late submissions will not be accepted, so be sure to submit your child's completed challenge by the deadline.

Prizes

Thanks to our PTA, we're able to offer two levels of rewards for student participation:

  • Complete ANY 6 challenges (MC 1–MC 12) — earn a small prize
  • Complete ALL 15 challenges — earn a certificate and either a special prize or a math celebration party (TBD based on participation and budget)

Parents are also encouraged to reward their kids at home, as our prize budget may be limited with high participation.
